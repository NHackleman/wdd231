Site Plan: Beginner's Guide to Urban Gardening

Project Overview: A beginner-friendly resource for city dwellers who want to start gardening in urban environments.

Site Name

Beginner's Guide to Urban Gardening

This name was selected because it clearly communicates the site’s purpose: to help beginners start gardening in urban settings. It’s approachable, descriptive, and easy to remember.

Optional domain availability checked: urbangardeningguide.com (or similar)

Site Purpose

The site provides step-by-step guidance, tips, and resources for individuals interested in starting their own urban gardens. It covers the basics of urban gardening, plant selection, container gardening, composting, pest control, and connects users to local resources.

Scenarios

  • What are the easiest plants to grow on a city balcony or rooftop?
  • How can I start composting in a small apartment?
  • Where can I find local resources or community gardens in my city?

Color Schema

Primary Green (#4CAF50): Used for headings, navigation, and key accents.
Accent Yellow (#FFD600): Used for highlights, buttons, and section backgrounds.
Background Off-White (#F8FFF5): Used for page background.

These colors were chosen to evoke a fresh, natural, and welcoming feel, reflecting the theme of gardening and growth.

Typography

  • Montserrat (Headings): Used for all main headings (h1, h2, h3) to provide a modern, bold, and approachable look.
  • Roboto (Body): Used for body text and paragraphs for readability and a clean appearance.

Both fonts are Google Fonts and widely supported for web use.
