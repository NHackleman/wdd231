Composting in Small Spaces

  • Use a small bin or bokashi system for kitchen scraps.
  • Balance greens (food waste) and browns (paper, leaves).
  • Avoid meat, dairy, and oily foods.

Pest Control

  • Inspect plants regularly for pests.
  • Use neem oil or insecticidal soap for infestations.
  • Encourage beneficial insects like ladybugs.
