Welcome!

This website is designed for city dwellers who want to start gardening but aren't sure where to begin. Explore easy-to-grow plants, learn about container gardening, composting, pest control, and connect with local resources.

Why Urban Gardening?

  • Grow your own fresh herbs and veggies
  • Improve your home's air quality
  • Connect with nature—even in a small space
  • Join a community of like-minded gardeners
